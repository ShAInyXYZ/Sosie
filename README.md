# Sosie

Sosie makes a lip-synced streamer facecam: a person talking to camera in the voice, look and words you choose.

Pick a **face** (any photo, or a character made with Qwen Image 2.1), a **voice** (Breeze TTS 2: cloned or designed), a **script** (typed or written by AI from your context) and a **look** (set, framing, format). Press **Generate**. You get an MP4 ready for the corner of a YouTube video or a Reel. Edit it in [MOceanX](../MOceanX).

Video: MiniMax H3 (Singularity v1.3 Ref2VA) with a speed LoRA, VEDA sparse attention and the fast VAE, on Sosie's own ComfyUI instance.

Status: in development. The build plan is [docs/MASTER_PROMPT.md](docs/MASTER_PROMPT.md); progress is tracked in [docs/PROGRESS.md](docs/PROGRESS.md).
